Privacy Policy for SimpleTranslator

Last updated: May 7, 2026

SimpleTranslator ("we", "our", or "us") provides voice and text translation features. This Privacy Policy explains what information we process and how we use it.

1. Information We Process

  • Voice input (microphone audio) when you use voice translation.
  • Text you type or speak for translation.
  • Language preferences and app settings stored on your device.
  • Basic technical logs needed to keep the app working.

2. How We Use Information

We use the information to:

  • Convert speech to text.
  • Translate text between selected languages.
  • Play translated speech.
  • Improve app stability and performance.

3. Data Sharing

We may send translation or speech content to third-party service providers (such as AI/translation or speech services) only to provide core app functions.

We do not sell personal data.

4. Data Retention

  • Translation history/settings may be stored locally on your device.
  • Server-side retention depends on the service providers used for translation/speech processing.

5. Permissions

SimpleTranslator requests:

  • Microphone permission: to capture voice for translation.
  • Speech recognition permission (where applicable): to transcribe your speech.

6. Children's Privacy

SimpleTranslator is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child provided personal information, contact us to request deletion.

7. Security

We use reasonable technical measures to protect data, but no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

8. Your Choices

You can stop using voice features at any time by denying microphone/speech permissions in device settings.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted at this URL with a revised "Last updated" date.
